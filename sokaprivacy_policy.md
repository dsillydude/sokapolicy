We do **not** require users to create an account to use the app. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or photos unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, by contacting us via email).

### 2. Automatically Collected Information

When you use the app, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

* Device type, model, and operating system version
* App usage data (such as screens viewed, interactions, and crash logs)
* IP address (used only for analytics, security, and service improvement)

This data is collected in an aggregated or anonymized form and is used solely to improve app performance and user experience.

### 3. Sports Content

soka Wp displays football and sports-related information obtained from trusted third-party data providers. Content such as live scores, fixtures, standings, team information, match statistics, and news is streamed over the internet. We do not access your personal photos, videos, files, or other device content without your explicit permission.

The app may request the following permissions:

* **Internet access:** To retrieve live scores, fixtures, news, and other sports content.
* **Notifications (optional):** To send match updates, score alerts, or other sports-related notifications if you choose to enable them.
